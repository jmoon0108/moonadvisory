About

An operator's perspective most M&A advisors don't have.

Moon Advisory provides independent advisory to founders, investors, and acquirers in AI and software across capital strategy, M&A, and corporate development, from fundraising through post-close integration.

The practice is led by Jamie Moon. Jamie has led M&A transactions up to ~$1B in enterprise value and 10+ venture investments, including Anthropic and CoreWeave, while heading Corporate Development & Ventures at Zoom. She also led the SafeBase investment through to its acquisition by Drata.

Most M&A advisors come from one vantage point, a bank or a PE firm. Jamie has sat in the operator's seat at companies that defined their categories during the AI buildout, structuring both venture investments and acquisitions from inside the businesses making the decisions.

"Traditional SaaS playbooks underprice or overprice AI businesses. Operating experience inside foundation model and AI infrastructure companies changes how you read the deal."
— On AI valuation diligence
